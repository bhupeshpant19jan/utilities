US Investment Area Discovery

Quick guide to first-run setup, workbook inputs, and interpreting ZIP-code rankings.

What this tool does

It discovers promising US markets, groups nearby ZIP codes into geographic clusters, and scores each ZIP for rental prospects, schools, safety, employment, affordability, and long-term resilience.

Scope: It answers “where should I look?” It does not find or underwrite individual houses. Property discovery remains a separate workflow.

First run: guided setup

The skill starts with a short conversational wizard. Existing preferences are pre-filled; you confirm or change them rather than entering everything again.

4. Methodology & AssumptionsWeights, gate definitions, thresholds, assumptions, sources, and report date.

How to interpret the results

Score (0–100) is the weighted ZIP-code score. Higher is stronger.

ScoreStrength
90–100Exceptional
80–89Very Strong
70–79Good
60–69Moderate
Below 60Weak

Qualification is separate from score:

  • Qualified: evidence supports all mandatory gates.
  • Conditional: promising, but one or more items need verification.
  • Failed: a mandatory gate is not met.
  • Insufficient Evidence: reliable information is not adequate to decide.

A ZIP scoring 88 can still fail if a mandatory school or safety gate is not met. Confidence indicates evidence quality: High (strong official or ZIP-/address-specific evidence), Medium (credible evidence with limitations), or Low (proxy, asking-rent, stale, or incomplete data).

School check: Elementary, middle, and high school ratings are checked individually—never averaged. School assignments, HOA rental rules, insurance, and property hazards often need a specific property address to confirm.

What to do next

Use the Executive Summary to choose clusters, compare ZIPs in the scorecard, then pass selected ZIP codes to the separate property-search workflow for listing-level rent comps, financing, cash flow, and due diligence.

Market screening is not financial, legal, tax, insurance, or school-placement advice. Verify current facts and address-specific conditions before making an investment decision.
